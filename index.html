Private, browser-based processing

Files are processed on your device when the selected tool supports local processing. Squeeze does not need an account to use the tools on this page. Some conversion libraries load from trusted content delivery networks, so an internet connection is required.

Frequently asked questions

Is Squeeze free to use?

The tools on this page are available to use without a paid account. Your device and browser capabilities may affect which operations work.

Does Squeeze upload my files?

Processing happens in your browser for the tools on this page. Your files remain on your device during supported local processing. Do not use the service for files if your browser or a future tool explicitly indicates that upload is required.

Which file formats are supported?

Image tools support JPG, PNG and WebP. PDF tools work with PDF files and selected Office or image formats for specific conversions. Audio and video formats depend on browser support.

Can I use Squeeze from any country?

Squeeze is a web-based tool and can be accessed internationally wherever the website and its required libraries are available. Performance depends on your internet connection, device and browser.

Files are processed on your device when supported. Internet is required to load conversion libraries. For best results, use current Chrome or Edge and avoid very large files.
