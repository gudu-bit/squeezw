Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how Novalyti ("we", "our" or "the website") handles information when you use https://squeezw.vercel.app/.

1. Files processed by browser tools

Image compression and the file tools on the homepage are designed to process selected files in your browser on your device. Files used by these local tools are not intentionally uploaded to Novalyti's server by the tool. Supporting scripts and libraries may load from third-party content delivery networks. Your browser, device and selected feature affect how processing works.

2. AI Writer and Humanizer

When you submit text to AI Writer or Humanizer, the text and the options you select (such as mode, tone and output language) are sent to our server so it can request an AI-generated result from OpenAI. Do not submit passwords, payment details, confidential business information, sensitive personal information or material you do not have permission to share. Uploaded PDF, DOCX or TXT files are read in your browser by the page; if you submit extracted text to the AI tool, that text is sent as described above.

AI requests are processed by OpenAI and are subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy and its applicable service terms. Novalyti does not intentionally create a user account or a personal document library for these requests. Technical logs or temporary processing data may still be handled by the hosting and AI service providers under their own policies.

3. Hosting, network and technical information

The website is hosted by Vercel. Like most websites, hosting and network providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time and error logs to deliver, secure and troubleshoot the service. Review Vercel's Privacy Policy for details.

4. Website analytics

Novalyti uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand general website usage, such as page visits and traffic patterns, and to improve the website. The analytics service is provided by Vercel and may process technical or usage information as described in Vercel Privacy Policy. Analytics is separate from the file-processing features.

5. Advertising and cookies

Advertising may be introduced in the future. If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalize ads, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements. This policy will be updated to describe the advertising technologies actually enabled on the site. Learn more in Google advertising policies.

6. Children's privacy

Novalyti is a general-purpose utility website and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Please do not submit children's personal or sensitive information to the AI tools.

7. Your choices and data safety

You can choose not to use the AI features and can avoid submitting sensitive information. For browser-based file tools, close the page after use and clear your browser's downloads or local data if needed. Always keep your own copy of important files and review generated results before relying on them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website's features or data practices change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
