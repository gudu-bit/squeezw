How to Compress a PDF Online and Reduce File Size

A PDF can become large because it contains high-resolution photos, scanned pages, embedded fonts or other content. Reducing its size can make it easier to email, upload to a form or store. The best approach depends on what is inside the document and how much visual quality you need to preserve.

Why is my PDF so large?

  • Scanned pages saved as high-resolution images.
  • Large photographs or graphics embedded in the document.
  • Many pages or multiple embedded resources.
  • Fonts and other data included to preserve document appearance.

Before compressing, keep a copy

Save an original copy before making changes. If the PDF is a contract, application, medical record or other important document, check that the compressed version remains readable and complete.

How to compress a PDF with Squeeze

  1. Open the Squeeze PDF tools.
  2. Choose the PDF tool and select your document.
  3. Select the compression option and available settings.
  4. Run the operation and download the output file.
  5. Open the new PDF and check text, images, page order and page count.

Other useful PDF tasks

Depending on what you need, Squeeze also provides tools to merge PDF files, split or extract pages, rotate pages and convert PDF pages to JPG or PNG. You can extract selectable text to formats such as TXT and Office files. Text extraction is not the same as recreating the original layout; tables, images and formatting may not be preserved. Scanned documents may require OCR before text can be extracted.

How much smaller will my PDF become?

There is no guaranteed compression percentage. A PDF that already contains optimized text and images may change very little. A document containing large images may have more room for reduction. Stronger image compression can make photographs look softer or text in scans harder to read.

Important: Do not use a compressed copy as your only archive. Review the output before sending it, especially when submitting official documents.

Frequently asked questions

Will compressing a PDF remove pages?

Compression should reduce file size, not intentionally remove pages. Always compare page count and content after processing.

Can I compress a scanned PDF?

Scanned PDFs commonly contain page images. Their size may be affected by image resolution and compression. Text extraction from a scan may require OCR.

Can I merge and split PDFs too?

Yes. Use the merge or split/extract options on the Squeeze PDF tools page.
