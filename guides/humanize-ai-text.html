AI WRITING GUIDE

How to Make AI-Written Text Sound More Natural

AI-assisted drafts can be useful starting points, but they may contain repeated phrases, generic transitions or sentences that do not sound like you. A good edit should make the writing clearer while keeping the facts, intended meaning and your own point of view.

1. Start with meaning, not word changes

Before rewriting, identify the main point of each paragraph. Remove sentences that repeat the same idea, but do not remove important qualifications, examples or context. A natural-sounding sentence is not useful if it changes what you meant.

2. Replace vague wording with specific details you can verify

Words such as “many benefits” or “in today's world” can make writing feel generic. Explain which benefit you mean and support factual statements with sources you have checked. Never invent statistics, quotations, citations or personal experiences to make a draft sound more convincing.

3. Vary sentence structure for readability

Use a mix of short and longer sentences where it helps the reader. Break up dense paragraphs. Prefer familiar words when they communicate the idea accurately. Avoid adding slang or contractions if the context calls for a formal tone.

4. Keep your own voice

Use terminology you understand and examples that genuinely relate to your experience. Read the result aloud. If a sentence does not sound like something you would say or write, revise it yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Does humanizing text guarantee it will pass an AI detector?

No. AI detectors can be inaccurate and their results are not a reliable guarantee of authorship. Squeeze's humanizer is intended to improve clarity and readability, not bypass detection systems.

Should I check AI-generated facts?

Yes. Verify factual claims, quotations, references, dates and figures against reliable sources. Rewriting does not make an incorrect statement true.

Can I choose a writing style?

The Squeeze AI Writer offers writing styles such as natural, formal, professional, casual, academic and friendly. Choose the one that suits your audience, then review the result.
