Tips for reducing PDF file size

PDF size depends on its contents. Documents containing high-resolution photos or scanned pages can be much larger than text-only documents. If your PDF contains images, reducing image resolution or quality may help, but can affect visual clarity.

Keep an original copy before compressing an important document. After processing, open the result and check that text, images, page order and layout are still suitable for your needs.

Online PDF tools in your browser

Frequently asked questions

Is the Squeeze PDF compressor free?

Yes. The PDF tools are offered free to use on the Squeeze website.

Can I compress a PDF online without installing software?

Yes. Open the PDF tab on Squeeze, choose the compression action, add your file and follow the on-screen steps.

Can I merge or split PDF files?

Yes. The PDF tools include options to merge documents and split or extract pages.

Can I convert PDF pages to JPG or PNG?

Yes. Choose the PDF-to-JPG or PDF-to-PNG action in the PDF tools.

Can I convert a PDF to Word or Excel?

Squeeze can export selectable PDF text to DOCX, XLSX and PPTX. These are text-based exports and may not reproduce the original layout. Scanned documents may not contain selectable text.

Will compression always make my PDF much smaller?

No. The amount of reduction depends on the original PDF and its contents. Some PDFs may already be optimised or contain mostly text.
